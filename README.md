# 🐾 Help Pebbles Get Her Surgery ❤️

This website tells the story of Pebbles, a beloved dog born in 2019 who suffered a terrible accident at the age of 3.

We created this website to share Pebbles' story and help raise funds for her medical care and surgery.

## ❤️ About Pebbles

Pebbles was born in 2019 and became a loved member of the family.

At the age of 3, she was involved in a serious accident that changed her life. We have been trying to get her the medical treatment and surgery she needs to have a better quality of life.

## 🙏 How You Can Help

You can help Pebbles by:

- Donating toward her treatment
- Sharing her story
- Telling others about her situation
